MediXplain

An explainable disease prediction prototype that combines a benchmarked scikit-learn model zoo, a FAISS-powered Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline, and an optional large-language-model narrative layer.

Best model: {{ best_model_name|replace('_', ' ')|title }} Retrieval backend: {{ backend|upper }} Diseases: {{ num_diseases }} Symptoms: {{ num_features }}

Multi-Model ML

Seven scikit-learn classifiers - Logistic Regression, Linear SVM, Bernoulli Naive Bayes, Decision Tree, Random Forest, AdaBoost, and MLP - are trained side by side. The winning model becomes the default predictor.

FAISS RAG

Paragraph-level knowledge is embedded with sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and served by a faiss.IndexFlatIP on normalised vectors. A TF-IDF fallback keeps the app runnable without FAISS.

LLM Explanations

An optional LLM call (OpenRouter or Gemini) turns the prediction and retrieved evidence into a short natural-language justification, with an explicit non-medical disclaimer.
